# 📸 Dashboard Images
 
This folder contains screenshots of the Power BI dashboard used in this project.

These visuals represent different stages of the customer lifecycle and business performance analysis.

---

## 📊 Images Included

### 1. Executive Overview (`overview.png`)
Provides a high-level summary of:
- Total customers  
- Total orders  
- Revenue trends  
- Average LTV  
- Retention rate  

👉 Used for quick business health monitoring

---

### 2. Acquisition & Channel Performance (`acquisition.png`)
Shows:
- Customer acquisition by channel  
- Marketing spend distribution  
- LTV/CAC ratio by channel  

👉 Helps evaluate marketing efficiency and channel quality

---

### 3. Customer Lifecycle (`lifecycle.png`)
Includes:
- Time to second purchase  
- Orders per customer  
- 30-day repeat rate  
- Customer segmentation  

👉 Used to understand repeat behavior and lifecycle delays

---

### 4. Retention Analysis (`retention.png`)
Displays:
- Cohort retention matrix  
- Monthly retention trends  
- Repeat rate across cohorts  

👉 Helps analyze long-term customer retention patterns

---

### 5. Activation Funnel (`funnel.png`)
Tracks customer journey:
- Session → Browsing → Cart → Checkout → Repeat  

Includes conversion rates at each stage.

👉 Identifies drop-offs and conversion bottlenecks

---

## 🎯 Purpose

These images are included to:
- Provide a quick visual understanding of the analysis  
- Showcase dashboard design and storytelling  
- Help recruiters review insights without opening Power BI
